Quick Buying Checklist

1. Understand your water: Water quality varies by location. Consider getting it tested before choosing a system.

2. Compare technologies: RO, UV and UF work differently. Select a system based on your water quality and needs.

3. Check maintenance: Review filter replacement schedules, servicing and ongoing costs.

4. Verify product claims: Look for reliable specifications and appropriate certifications.
